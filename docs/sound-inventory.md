# Sound inventory

Fog sounds like a small European town in steady autumn rain. Use this list to gather individual cues that can be mixed, panned, and faded in the scene. Quiet stretches between events give the rain room to carry the soundscape.

## Variations and playback

Collect **at least three distinct recordings or takes of every cue when available**. Group takes by what they depict. Wet cobblestone footsteps, for example, form one group with three or more versions of the same sound. Shuffle the group, play the next take each time the cue triggers, and reshuffle after the cycle. With multiple takes, start the next cycle with a different take from the one that just played.

Choose a rain-bed variation at random when playback starts and loop it continuously. Keep it running through building, street, and town regeneration. All other cues fade out with the incoming fog and back in during the reveal.

## Generic environment

| Cue | What to gather |
| --- | --- |
| **Soft steady rain** | A seamless stereo bed with a stable, gentle level. This is the continuous layer for the whole scene. |
| **Rain on leaves** | A softer, close texture to place beneath tree canopies and beside hedges. |
| **Eaves and gutter drips** | Small, spaced drops off a roof edge or downspout. A light trickle can mark a particularly wet corner. |
| **Puddle drops** | Isolated drops and short ripples of little plops near the ground. |
| **Breeze through foliage** | Brief, low rustles from trees, shrubs, and damp fallen leaves. |
| **Distant birds** | Sparse individual calls for quieter garden streets and outskirts. |

Place the drips, rustles, and other detail cues at their sources. Mix them as local activity so the soft rain bed carries the scene during a fog reveal.

## Street-specific sounds

| Cue | What to gather |
| --- | --- |
| **Wet stone footsteps** | Short walking passes on rain-darkened cobbles or paving stones. |
| **Wet pavement footsteps** | Short walks on damp asphalt or concrete, with occasional light splashes. |
| **Garden and path footsteps** | Separate groups for wet gravel and damp fallen leaves. |
| **Passing car** | One slow car approaching and receding, with a brief wet-tire hiss. |
| **Passing bicycle** | A soft tire roll or a brief chain sound. |
| **Passing bus** | A brief diesel roll and wet-road sound for through routes. |
| **Passing people** | A short, indistinct adult murmur for a small group walking by. |

| Street profile | Typical selection |
| --- | --- |
| **Old town** | Wet cobblestone steps, occasional passing adults, a rare car or bicycle. |
| **Civic square** | Steps across broad paving, small groups at the edges of the square, vehicles on its through road. |
| **Terraced neighborhood** | Wet pavement steps, the occasional puddle splash, a slow car passing the row. |
| **Garden street** | Damp leaves and gravel steps near entrances, a bicycle or an infrequent car. |
| **Village edge** | Gravel and wet-road steps, longer quiet gaps, a single car or bus on the through route. |

Attach footsteps and traffic to moving sources on the street. Prefer centered recordings so playback can pan them across the scene as they pass the listener.

## Building and public-site sounds

Use a building's active use to choose cues. A café with flats can play café sounds at its ground-floor entrance and different household sounds from an upstairs window. Share cue groups across uses where they fit, such as a simple door latch across several kinds of housing.

| Building or site | Local cues |
| --- | --- |
| **Detached house** | Door latch, soft steps at the entrance, faint household murmur. |
| **Semi-detached pair** | Two entrances that can play door and doorstep sounds independently. |
| **Terraced house** | Door opening or closing, footsteps on a step, voices softened through a window. |
| **Flats or walk-up** | Shared entrance door, stairwell footfalls, distant household voices. |
| **Shop house with flats** | Gentle shop-door chime and low counter chatter, with separate upstairs household cues. |
| **Café with flats** | Low adult chatter, brief adult laughter, a short milk-steamer hiss, a cup set on a saucer. |
| **Restaurant with flats or rooms** | Low dining-room murmur and the clink of a plate or cutlery. |
| **Bookshop with flats** | Soft door or chime, a page turning, a chair shifting. |
| **Bakery** | Paper bag rustle, a tray sliding, quiet counter activity. |
| **Grocer** | Paper bags, a crate set down, muted customers at the counter. |
| **Small office** | Quiet adult conversation, a chair shifting or steps near the entrance. |
| **Library** | A page turn, a chair moving, or a faint cluster of hushed voices. |
| **Church** | Bell tolls with a natural decay, distant choral chanting, short organ passages heard through the building. |
| **School** | Muffled groups of children talking behind windows or across a yard, light footsteps near the entrance. |
| **Town hall** | Heavy entrance door, steps in the hall, subdued adult voices. |
| **Clinic** | Door opening, footsteps and a quiet waiting-room murmur. |
| **Guesthouse** | Door latch, low lobby voices, an occasional bag rolling over paving. |
| **Workshop** | A few restrained tool taps, a short scrape, or movement behind a workroom door. |
| **Market hall** | Diffuse adult murmur, stall activity, a box or basket set down. |
| **Park pavilion** | Footsteps beneath the shelter and a short cluster of voices at the pavilion. |
| **Park** | Children's laughter and joyful calls, with space between groups of sounds. |
| **Bus stop** | Brake hiss, doors opening or closing, brief idle and pull-away when a bus stops. |

Choose voices that read through cadence and tone, with words blending into a murmur. Gather short, isolated events with room for their tails. In the mix, soften distant sources and sounds heard through doors, glass, or walls.

## First prototype

Start with the soft rain bed, wet pavement steps, a slow car pass, café murmur and milk steamer, a quiet bookshop door, and park laughter. Gather and label the variations in each group so the café-to-bookshop reveal can change the local sound while the rain continues.

Record the creator, original source URL, license, and any edits with each collected file. Use original recordings or CC0 material so the pack can be shared with the project.
