# Fog sound catalog

Recording pages checked on 19 November 2026. The page-derived rankings are provisional. Every recording still needs a listening check.

Keep one soft stereo rain bed running through fog reveals. Pan short, centered excerpts as local sources, fade them with the town mix and soften distant or enclosed sources. Leave enough quiet between events for rain to carry the scene. Shuffle distinct takes within each cue and avoid repeating the last take at the start of the next shuffle. [Cue checklist](docs/sound-inventory.md) · [Mixing context](docs/soundscape-and-sound-engine.md) · [JSON catalog](sound-catalog.json)

Each verified entry records its file page and license label. The file’s CC0 permission covers editing, commercial use and sharing the recording itself. Freesound originals are available through an account and are marked for manual download in the JSON. The BigSoundBank WAV links are eligible for the downloader.
